# Research corpus

Claims taken from Nelson's active research projects, not from textbook
exercises: Kondo two-orbital hierarchy, general relativity energy identities
and hollow-core shells, sudden mass quench of a free scalar, relativistic
rocket bound, harmonic-gradient identity, SU(2)/SU(3) gauge checks and the
Mobius-strip Nagaoka theorem. Every claim's truth value is measured by the
scripts in `reference/` (SymPy Einstein tensors from scratch, 32-dimensional
Jordan-Wigner matrices, sparse exact diagonalization) before it enters the
corpus; their deposited outputs live in `reference/outputs/`. A case is kept
only when the printed value of its `metadata.reference` key matches its
`expected` field. `metadata.provenance` points at the project file the claim
came from; `metadata.seeded_error` names the single alteration in each false
claim. Natario-bound claims are left out on purpose: the paper is under
review.

## v1.2 (2026-10-01): one proposition per case

`cycle/research_v1_2.json`, generated by `build_research_v1_2.py` from
shared definition blocks so every case of a family carries the same
conventions verbatim. 50 cases: 29 true, 21 seeded false.

| Family | Cases | True | False |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Kondo norms and exchange commutators | 14 | 8 | 6 |
| Planar shift wall | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| Static shell with p_r = 0 | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| Kinnersley photon rocket | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Unit-lapse shell | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| Sudden mass quench | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| Relativistic rocket | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Harmonic gradient | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| SU(2) curvature, SU(3) spectrum | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Mobius Nagaoka | 2 | 1 | 1 |

Why: in the v1.1 measurement (`docs/evidence/RESEARCH_CLAIMS_COMPARISON_20260930.md`,
section 5) every remaining INCONCLUSIVE verdict came from a case that bundled
several propositions; the validator tested one and the re-anchored analyst
correctly refused to certify the rest. Each bundled claim is now split
(`metadata.split_from` names the v1.1 case) and each block of true claims
gained seeded-false siblings so the false-acceptance rate keeps a comparable
base. Regenerate and verify with:

```powershell
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe benchmarks\quality\research\build_research_v1_2.py --check
```

Run (production arm, local oracle, re-anchored scoring):

```powershell
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\run_quality_benchmarks.py --case-root benchmarks\quality\research --no-legacy --tier standard --tracks cycle --oracle local --cycle-timeout 3000 --config no-ensemble
```

Heavy cases carry their own oracle `timeout` (900 s for the symbolic Einstein
tensors, 600 s for the diagonalization); the runner widens the cycle budget to
`timeout + 900` s for them. At the v1.1 pace of about 8 min per production
cycle, the full corpus is roughly 6.5 h of wall clock.

## History

`history/v1_1/cycle/research_v1.json` is the 27-case corpus of the measured
runs (v1 wording on 2026-09-30 / 2026-10-01 night, v1.1 wording on the
2026-10-01 morning re-run; reports under
`docs/evidence/research_arms_20261001/`). It sits two levels deep so the main
root does not load it; reproduce those runs with
`--case-root benchmarks\quality\research\history\v1_1`.

v1.1 fixed two wording defects both arms exposed, marked in
`metadata.revised`: the static-shell DEC equivalence needs `m' > 0` (at a
radius with `m' = 0` both rho and p_perp vanish and the DEC holds for any
`2m/R < 1`; `reference/gr_reference.py` prints the loophole as
`gr_static_shell_v1_wording_false_at_m_prime_zero: TRUE`), and the Kondo
cases reused `b`, `c` as both cross-product and spinor indices (now `j`, `k`).
